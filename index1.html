8-TREASURE RICE PUDDING

Steam the glutinous rice (first steam),Mix the rice with coconut oil and sugar and Spread the toppings on the bottom of the bowl. Add a layer of cooked glutinous rice and Add the red bean paste layer Fill up the bowl with the rest of the rice Steam the second time, then drizzle with the syrup Plate the rice and serve

INGREDIENTS: 1 1/2 cups (360 ml) glutinous rice 2 tablespoons coconut oil (or shortening) 1 tablespoon sugar 4 tablespoons red bean paste (Homemade or Store-bought) 1 cup water 2 tablespoons sugar 1 heaping tablespoon dried chrysanthemum buds 1 teaspoon cornstarch 3 jujubes 1 tablespoon golden raisins 1 tablespoon pumpkin seeds 1/2 tablespoon goji berries 1 walnut half
